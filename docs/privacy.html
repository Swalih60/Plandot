Plandot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version
  • Plandot has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your tasks, notes and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • If you buy Plandot Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, and our purchase-processing provider, RevenueCat, receives the purchase receipt so we can unlock Pro for you.

1. Who we are

Plandot (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is made by an independent developer based in India. If you have any questions about this policy, email support@EXAMPLE.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is saved in a local file inside the app’s private storage on your phone. This includes your tasks and their titles, notes, subtasks, priorities, tags, due dates and times, repeat rules, completion history, daily notes, reminder times, statistics and your settings. That data is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Because the data exists only on your device, uninstalling the app or losing your phone will delete it, unless you have made a backup.

3. Backups you export

You can choose to export a backup as a JSON file and import it later. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it (for example, your Files app, a cloud drive or a messaging app). The provider you choose handles the file under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

4. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your device. No notification server is involved, and we do not receive any information when a reminder fires. You can turn notification permission off at any time in your device settings.

5. Purchases (Plandot Pro)

Plandot Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment. We never see your card details, name, email address or billing address.

To check and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a third-party service that processes in-app purchases on our behalf. When you open the paywall, buy, or restore a purchase, the app talks to RevenueCat, and RevenueCat receives:

DataWhy
A random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name, email or any account)To link your purchase to your installation and let you restore it
Purchase history and receipt or transaction data from Apple or Google (product, price, currency, purchase and expiry dates, trial status)To validate the purchase, prevent fraud and unlock Pro
Basic technical data sent with network requests: IP address, device model, operating system and app version, store country and localeTo operate the service and show the correct local prices

We use the RevenueCat dashboard only to see aggregated purchase and subscription figures (for example, how many active subscriptions exist). We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with any other data about you. RevenueCat’s handling of this data is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Apple’s and Google’s own policies cover the payment itself: Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

When you are online, the app checks with RevenueCat at startup whether you have Pro and loads the current prices, including on the free version. Only the anonymous ID, any purchase data and the technical data listed above are sent. Your tasks and notes are never included. All features of the free version work fully offline.

6. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no collection of your name, email or phone number
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers (IDFA / Android advertising ID)
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No location, contacts, photos, health-data or microphone access
  • No selling or sharing of personal data for advertising, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites

7. Data retention and deletion

On-device data: you control it completely. Delete individual tasks in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Purchase records: RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Apple and Google keep their own records of your transactions. To ask us to delete the purchase record linked to your anonymous ID from RevenueCat, email us with the store order or transaction ID shown on your receipt. Deleting it may stop “Restore purchases” from working until you restore from the store again.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and US state privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Since almost all of your data never leaves your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For purchase data held by RevenueCat, contact us at the address above and we will respond within the time the law requires.

9. Children

Plandot is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Purchases on a child’s device are subject to the parental controls of Apple or Google.

10. Security

Your task data is stored in the app’s private storage, which the operating system protects, and is encrypted at rest when your device uses device encryption (the default on modern iPhones and Android phones protected by a passcode). Communication with RevenueCat uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Exported backup files are plain JSON and are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change (for example, if a new feature sends data off your device), we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date before that version of the app is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
